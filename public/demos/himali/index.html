IIThe Artisan's Hand

From the loom
to your door.

Each HIMALI piece passes through three pairs of hands before it reaches yours. We pay every artisan fairly, and we name them.

  1. I

    Handwoven in Nepal

    Cloth is woven on wooden looms in Palpa, Bhaktapur and the high valleys — a sari can take three weeks.

  2. II

    Finished by Artisans

    Master tailors in Kathmandu cut, embroider and hand-finish every hem, then sign the label inside.

  3. III

    Delivered to Korea

    Wrapped in hand-made lokta paper and flown to Seoul — at your door in 7 to 12 days, duties included.
