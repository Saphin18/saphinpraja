A Guide · 네팔 의상 가이드

The Nepali Wardrobe

Five pieces worth knowing — and how to wear them in Seoul.

कुर्थाKurtha
A long, easy tunic worn with suruwal trousers and a shawl. Nepal’s everyday staple — lovely with denim, too.
सारीSari
Five to six metres of unstitched cloth, draped. For weddings, festivals and evenings out.
गुन्यू चोलोGunyu Cholo
A wrapped blouse (cholo) and skirt (gunyu) — the traditional dress of Nepal’s hill communities.
ढाकाDhaka
A geometric handwoven cloth from Palpa, made thread by thread on wooden looms.
पश्मिनाPashmina
Fine Himalayan goat wool, feather-light and warm — made for Korean winters.
